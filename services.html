Three ways to work with FleetEdge

Each engagement is scoped to what your business actually needs — a one-time audit, a full reorder system build, or ongoing advisory once the system's running.

SERVICE 01

Diagnostic Audit

Start here if you know something's off but can't pinpoint what. We go through your existing data and tell you exactly where the problem is — and whether it's worth fixing.

What's included

Full review of stock levels, sell-through, and margin against your POS or spreadsheet data. A findings memo ranking issues by cash impact, not guesswork.

Typical length

1–2 weeks, depending on data quality and how many locations or SKUs are involved.

Best for

Businesses that want a clear-eyed read on what's actually happening before committing to a bigger fix.

SERVICE 02

Reorder System Design

The core engagement. We build reorder points, safety stock levels, and a simple restocking process sized to your actual demand — implementable in the tools you already use.

What's included

SKU-level reorder points and safety stock, an ABC/XYZ segmentation so you know what deserves attention, and a restocking workflow your team can run without a consultant in the room.

Typical length

3–6 weeks, including a short pilot period to confirm the logic holds up against real sales.

Best for

Businesses ready to stop reordering on gut feel and want a system, not another spreadsheet nobody updates.

SERVICE 03

Ongoing Advisory

For businesses that want a second set of eyes on the numbers month to month — without hiring a full-time analyst.

What's included

Monthly review of inventory health, margin trends, and reorder performance, with a short memo flagging what needs attention before it becomes a problem.

Typical length

Ongoing, month to month — cancel anytime once the system's stable enough to run on its own.

Best for

Businesses past the initial build who want accountability without adding headcount.

THE TOOLKIT

What we actually use to get there

No fixed methodology — we pick what fits the problem, and we name it so you can follow the reasoning.

SCOR-based diagnosis

For structural issues spanning plan, source, make, and deliver — used when the problem isn't just inventory math.

ABC / XYZ segmentation

To separate what deserves tight control from what doesn't — so attention goes where the cash impact is.

Statistical safety stock & EOQ

The math behind reorder points and order quantities, sized to your real demand variability and lead times.
