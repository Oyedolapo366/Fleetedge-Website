THE PATTERN

Two opposite problems, showing up at once

This is what we see walking into most small and mid-sized retail and inventory-heavy businesses for the first time.

01 / SHELF

Stockouts on the items customers actually want

Fast movers sit at zero while nobody's watching sell-through — so every empty shelf is a sale that happened somewhere else.

→
02 / CASH

Cash frozen in stock that isn't moving

Meanwhile, other categories are stacked well past what the shelf needs — tying up money that could fund the products actually selling.

→
03 / TRUST

Customers quietly switching stores

Reliability is what turns a walk-in into a regular. Two disappointments in a row, and most customers stop checking.

→
HOW WE WORK

One route, three stops

No enterprise rollout, no six-month engagement. A clear path from "what's actually going on" to a system you run yourself.

STOP 01 — DIAGNOSE

Find out what's actually happening

We audit your existing data — POS, spreadsheets, whatever you're using — to find the real cause of stockouts and overstock, not just the symptom on the shelf.

STOP 02 — DESIGN

Build the reorder logic

Reorder points and safety stock sized to your actual demand and lead times — implementable in the tools you already have.

STOP 03 — RUN

Hand you a system you can operate

You leave with a process your team can run without us — plus a light-touch check-in cadence if you want ongoing eyes on it.
