Retail / Grocery CASE FE-A

Situation

A multi-category retail store was restocking on gut feel. Fast-moving items regularly ran out while other categories sat well past what the shelf needed — tying up cash the owner didn't realize was frozen.

Approach

An audit of the existing point-of-sale data to segment SKUs by how often they sold and how much cash they were sitting on. Reorder points and low-stock thresholds were rebuilt category by category.

Outcome

The owner moved from reactive restocking to a threshold-based system they run themselves, with clear visibility into which categories are over-invested versus under-stocked at a glance.

Field Asset Tracking CASE FE-B

Situation

A business managing physical equipment across multiple field locations had no consistent way to see what was deployed, in storage, or unaccounted for — visibility gaps that cost as much as stock gaps do in retail.

Approach

Built a live inventory tracker and dashboard consolidating asset status across sites, replacing a patchwork of spreadsheets that hadn't been reconciled in months.

Outcome

Leadership gained a single source of truth for asset location and status, cutting down time spent chasing down where equipment actually was.
