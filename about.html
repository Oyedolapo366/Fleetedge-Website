Built to think like the businesses we work with

FleetEdge Logix Consulting Ltd. is an independent inventory, logistics, and supply chain consulting practice based in Lagos, serving small and mid-sized businesses across retail and asset-based operations.

THE APPROACH

Peer-level thinking, not consultant-speak

Most SMBs don't need an enterprise-grade platform or a six-month engagement — they need someone who can walk the floor, read the data honestly, and hand over a system they can actually run. That's the whole premise FleetEdge is built on.

We name the framework we're using, flag our assumptions before they go unchecked, and favor a phased plan — quick wins first, structural fixes second — over a theoretical best-practice dump nobody has the staff to implement.

HOW WE THINK

No fixed playbook — the right tool for the problem

01

Symptoms are not root causes

An empty shelf and a full one can point to the same broken process. We diagnose before we prescribe.

02

Precision and auditability matter

Every recommendation comes with the calculation behind it — formula, inputs, result — so it can be defended to a CFO, a bank, or a skeptical co-founder.

03

Cash flow and staff bandwidth are real constraints

A technically elegant fix that requires headcount you don't have isn't a fix. We size recommendations to what you can actually run.

04

Momentum over perfect information

Where data is incomplete, we say exactly what's missing rather than quietly filling gaps with assumptions — but we don't stall waiting for perfect data either.
